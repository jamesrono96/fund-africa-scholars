## Important exclusions and accuracy rules

- Do not present a partial tuition discount as fully funded.
- Do not generalise a country-restricted or identity-restricted award to all Africans.
- Do not treat an admissions deadline as a scholarship deadline.
- Do not assume that a university scholarship covers Computer Science just because the university offers that degree.
- For every entry, verify the 2027 intake, course, nationality, award value, application route and deadline against the live official provider page.
- Keep expired, superseded or uncertain opportunities in a research/archive state, not in the public directory.
