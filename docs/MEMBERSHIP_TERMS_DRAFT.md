# Membership terms — owner policy draft

**Status:** Draft for jurisdiction-specific legal review before publication  
**Product:** Fund Africa Scholars  
**Price:** USD $5 one-time payment

## 1. Membership price and duration
A lifetime membership costs USD $5 as a single payment. It is not a recurring subscription, has no automatic renewal and does not expire after a fixed number of months. The payment provider may display or settle an equivalent amount in another supported currency; any conversion, taxes or provider fees must be disclosed at checkout before the customer confirms payment.

## 2. Free access
Visitors may browse, search, view scholarship details and follow official scholarship application links without paying.

## 3. Lifetime member features
After the platform verifies a successful payment and activates the account, lifetime members receive:
- Save/bookmark scholarships.
- Application tracking.
- Deadline watch and in-dashboard reminders.
- Private application notes.
- Enhanced personalised recommendations.

The features are provided as described in the live product. Scholarship availability, eligibility, deadlines and provider decisions are controlled by the scholarship provider, not Fund Africa Scholars.

## 4. No voluntary refunds; mandatory legal rights preserved
The operator's policy is that all membership sales are final and **no voluntary refunds are offered** once a payment has been successfully processed and membership access has been granted.

This policy does not remove, restrict or override any refund, cancellation, chargeback, consumer-protection or other remedy that applicable law makes mandatory or non-waivable. If the law applicable to a customer's purchase requires a refund or other remedy in a particular circumstance, the operator will comply with that law. Payment-provider dispute and card-network rights are also unaffected.

Before checkout is enabled, the operator must obtain jurisdiction-specific legal advice on whether any pre-contract disclosure, digital-content consent, cooling-off period or other mandatory consumer right applies.

## 5. Payment and activation
Membership is activated only after the platform receives and verifies the payment provider's authoritative successful-payment confirmation. A browser redirect, screenshot, email forwarded by a customer or client-side setting does not establish payment. Pending, failed, reversed, refunded or disputed payments do not qualify as successful payment for entitlement purposes; any suspension or revocation must follow provider records and applicable law.

## 6. Account responsibility and support
Members are responsible for protecting their sign-in credentials and keeping their account email current. Support contact details and expected response times must be published before paid checkout is enabled. Do not send passwords, one-time codes or payment-card details to support.

## 7. Service availability and scholarship information
The platform aims to provide useful discovery and tracking tools but does not award scholarships, make admission decisions or guarantee funding. Scholarship information can change; users should verify all requirements and deadlines with the official provider before applying. The service may undergo maintenance and improvements.

## 8. Privacy and termination
Personal information must be handled under the published privacy notice and applicable law. Any account suspension, termination, data retention or deletion process must be described in the final terms and privacy notice and must comply with applicable law.

## 9. Operator details and governing law
Before publication, complete the operator's legal name, business/contact address, support email, effective date, governing law, dispute process and any mandatory local disclosures. These details must not be invented.
