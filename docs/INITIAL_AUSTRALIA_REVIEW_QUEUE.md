## Publication gate

Do not import or publish any candidate from this file solely because it appears here. For each record, the administrator must:

1. Confirm the live official provider page and that the opportunity is current for the intended intake.
2. Confirm nationality eligibility for the student's country.
3. Confirm the exact undergraduate Computer Science / IT course is eligible.
4. Confirm the award value, duration and whether it covers tuition only or other costs.
5. Confirm the deadline or clearly state that no separate scholarship deadline is published.
6. Confirm the application route and whether a separate scholarship application is required.
7. Save evidence URLs and reviewer notes in the platform's verification checklist, then mark verified and published only after all required checks are complete.

**Student-facing accuracy rule:** A tuition fee reduction must never be labelled “fully funded”. Admission to a degree and scholarship eligibility are separate decisions. Students must confirm current terms with the provider.
